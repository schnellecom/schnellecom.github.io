Simplicial Surfaces is a package for GAP. I initially rewrote parts of its visualization and manual-generation functionality as part of the "Fachpraktikum / Practical Training Algebra" and continued developing it as a student and graduate assistant at the Chair of Algebra and Representation Theory at RWTH Aachen University.
