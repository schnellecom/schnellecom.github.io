As part of my position at the Institute of Structural Mechanics and Lightweight Design (SLA), I am involved in the [DFG Collaborative Research Centre / Transregio 188 (SFB/TRR 188)](https://trr188.de/index.php/en/) – Damage Controlled Forming Processes.

Specifically, I am working in Subproject A09, which focuses on damage mechanics-based process design for the damage-controlled forming of hybrid components.
