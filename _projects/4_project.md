Specifically, I am working in [Subproject A04](https://www.sfbtrr280.de/en/research/project-area-a/subproject-a04/), where we focus on the mathematical and geometric foundations for topological interlocking and material-minimized construction. 

Previously, I worked on a seedfund project related to Simplicial Surfaces within the SFB. I continue my collaboration with the researchers from that project to apply our algebraic and geometric insights to structural engineering.
